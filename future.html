Our future

I think about our future more than you probably realise. (I know it's still early, but I can't help it.)

Not because I have everything planned out, but because I love imagining all the little moments we'll get to share one day.

I want to explore the world with you. I want us to go to places we've never been before, get lost together, take too many photos, try foods we've probably never even heard of, and come home with stories we'll tell to our grandchildren.

I want a home that feels like us. Somewhere we can decorate together, argue over what colour the walls should be, leave little notes for each other, and slowly fill with memories. I don't care if it's a tiny apartment or a big house - I just want it to feel like home because you're there.

I want to marry you one day. Not because of the wedding or the rings, but because I want to spend my life choosing you over and over again. I want to stand there knowing that no matter what happens, we're facing it together.

I want a family with you someday. I want to watch you become the kind of dad I already know you'll be (aka da best). I want our kids to grow up knowing what real love looks like because they'll see it every day nonstop.

I want to drink hot chocolate with you on a christmas night as we watch a marathon of christmas movies.

I want to grow old with you.

I want grey hair, wrinkles, bad eyesight, and silly arguments over what movie we've already watched three times. I want to laugh at things nobody else understands because we've spent decades collecting inside jokes.

Most of all, I hope that years from now, when we're living the life we once dreamed about, we'll look at each other and remember that it all started with two people who crossed paths in dahood (im crine son).

No matter where life takes us, as long as I'm walking through it with you, I'll already be exactly where I want to be Riley ♡
